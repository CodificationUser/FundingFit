The bank agrees to lend a principal amount of EUR 80,000. The loan will be repaid over a term of 60 months by equal monthly instalments of principal and interest.
Interest accrues at a fixed nominal annual rate of 4.20% for the entire term.
An arrangement fee of EUR 1,200 is paid separately at drawdown and is not financed in or withheld from the principal.
Other costs: none.
