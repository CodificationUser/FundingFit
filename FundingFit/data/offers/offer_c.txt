FUNDINGFIT DEMONSTRATION TERM SHEET
Offer C
Entirely synthetic example for a small business buying equipment.
Loan currency: EUR.
Capital to be borrowed: EUR 100000.
Annual nominal interest: 3.90%.
Interest basis: fixed rate, unchanged until maturity.
Time to repay: 60 months.
The principal and interest are repaid through equal monthly payments in arrears.
Initial application fee: EUR 1500.
The initial fee is paid separately at disbursement, outside the loan amount.
No insurance is charged.
No guarantee fee is charged.
No broker fee is charged.
Other costs are none.
The loan has no payment holiday, balloon repayment, or interest deferral.
