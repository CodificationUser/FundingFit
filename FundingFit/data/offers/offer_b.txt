Offer B
Fictional demonstration: no real lender is represented.
The facility finances business equipment.
All payments are denominated in EUR.

This is a fixed-rate loan throughout its life.

Payments are equal monthly instalments in arrears.

This fee is paid separately when funds are released and is not borrowed.
Insurance charges: none.
Guarantee charges: none.
Brokerage charges: none.
There are no other costs.
There is no grace period, final balloon, or interest deferral.
